How to run a sweep

1. Open this page in Safari. Ring/silent switch to ring, volume up. Tap Enable sensors & sound, allow motion, Test beep.
2. Strap the phone flat to the plywood beside the device, same place and orientation for the whole sweep.
3. Set up the sweep, then tap Measure noise with everything still.
4. For each trial: reset the detector, tap Start trial, shake. A rising beep means you're on target; keep it steady. A long high tone means done: stop shaking.
5. Answer Detected or Not detected. Use Redo if something went wrong (bumped device, wrong level).
6. At the end, Save all (.zip) or Share all… (AirDrop to your Mac). Results are also kept on this phone until you start a new sweep.
Low levels are small motions: 0.02 g at 1 Hz is only about 5 mm each way; at 2 Hz about 1 mm. Shake slowly for the low levels.
