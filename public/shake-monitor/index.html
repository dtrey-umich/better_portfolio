Checklist

1. Open this page in Safari (not inside another app). Ring/silent switch to ring, volume up.
2. Tap Enable sensors & sound and allow motion access, then Test beep.
3. Strap the phone flat to the plywood beside the device, same place and orientation each run.
4. Check the reading at rest is well below the target (it is your noise floor).
5. Start recording run, shake until it beeps, keep the level steady for the required duration, then Stop run.
6. Save CSV or Share CSV… (AirDrop, Mail, Files) after every run.
